# Research: Game Engagement and Retention (2024-2026)

This is the evidence base for the v2 rewrite of the three skills in this plugin — `engagement-retention-advisor`, `retention-strategy-designer` and `interaction-reward-moments` — and it exists because most of what the v1 skills assert either settled differently between 2023 and 2026 or was never evidence in the first place. Nine documents cover player psychology and reward moments, retention metrics and benchmarks, onboarding and sessions, LiveOps and lifecycle, genre and title lessons, regulation and platform policy, non-game domains, the Korean market, and the packaging constraints of the plugin itself. It was produced by 46 research agents returning 264 findings against 250 opened sources, after which 14 load-bearing claims were routed to adversarial verification; 20 of those 28 verdicts came back with corrections, which is the single strongest argument for reading the tags before quoting the numbers. Every figure travels with its population, its definition and its data year, because in this literature the population moves the number more than the design does. Where the evidence is genuinely split, the document carries both readings rather than picking one. Where a number does not exist publicly, the document says so, and the skills must refuse to invent one. Nothing here is a target until a definition gate has been passed.

Read a claim's tag before quoting it:

- **[corrected]** — the first recorded form of this claim was wrong. What appears here is the corrected form; the earlier version is a "do not quote" item.
- **[contested]** — real evidence points both ways. Carry both readings into the skill; never resolve one away.
- **[unverified]** — the primary was not opened or could not be reached. Usable as context, never as a target, a benchmark or a compliance statement.
- **[unknown] / verified absence** — the figure does not exist publicly. The skill's correct output is "no such benchmark exists", not an estimate.
- **[secondary] / [low confidence] / [preprint]** — provenance downgrades: a real source with weaker standing than the sentence around it implies.

## Executive summary

The fifteen findings that most change what the skills say.

1. Curiosity about one's own action success — not enjoyment — is the only predictor of continued play; enjoyment did not predict playtime at all (Kao, Ballou, Gerling, Breitsohl & Deterding, CHI 2024, n=1,699 — 02a §6).
2. Self-reported experience does not forecast behaviour, from two independent directions: need satisfaction showed weak or null association with subsequent play, session length and return latency in trace data, and CSDT withdrew BANGS citing lost predictive validity (Ballou et al. 2026 preprint, 555 players / 11k daily observations — 02a §2.4).
3. "Dopamine point" is not a defensible product term: the RPE account's original form is "probably too simple" and dopamine mediates wanting, not liking (Gershman et al., *Nature Neuroscience* 27:1645–1655, 2024; Robinson & Berridge, *Annu. Rev. Psychol.* 76, 2025 — 02a §1).
4. Variable-ratio scheduling does not replicate as an engagement lever; what does move money is odds opacity — censoring exact odds raised willingness-to-pay ~45%, curated samples ~45%, both together +100% (Young et al., *Perspectives on Behavior Science* 2026; Cordes, Dertwinkel-Kalt & Werner, *JEBO* 2024 — 02a §7).
5. Juice is not a volume knob: success-dependent amplified feedback raised competence and curiosity while non-contingent amplification *lowered* effectance (−.19) and competence (−.43), and both "None" and "Extreme" dose reduce playtime (Kao et al., CHI 2024, n=1,699; Kao 2020, N=3,018 — 02a §5).
6. A retention number is a definition before it is a fact — the same app reads 43% or 32% D1 on the day convention alone, published D30 for "mobile games" spans 0.68% to 17.8%, and the actual market median is 22 / just under 4 / 0.68–0.79%, not 40/20/10 (Amplitude 2025; GameAnalytics 2026, 16,262 games ≥1,000 MAU — 02b §1–§4).
7. Tutorials pay only in complex games (+29% playtime in the most complex of three, null in the two simpler, and documented cases of harm), and the 9-minute first-session rule is a between-*game* correlation whose own author said short sessions are a symptom of churn rather than a cause (Andersen et al., CHI 2012, 45,000+ players; deltaDNA 2016, 275 games — 02c §2–§3).
8. No vendor publishes FTUE or tutorial-completion benchmarks at all, so tutorial-step drop-off must be an internal instrument, not a benchmarked target (GameAnalytics 2025/2026, Liftoff, Sensor Tower — verified absence, 02c §1.3).
9. Difficulty relief for at-risk players is the best-evidenced retention intervention in the set: Retention-1/7/14 up +2.7 / +2.5 / +2.0 pp and net +$0.07 per average user, despite lowering purchases in the eased round (Ascarza, Netzer & Runge, *IJRM* 42(4):975–995, 2025, ~330,000-player 12-week RCT — 02b §6, 02d §10).
10. "Reward return, never punish absence" stopped being an ethics preference and became a rating price: daily quests that reward returning are PEGI 7, mechanisms that punish non-return by losing content or reducing progress are PEGI 12 for games submitted from June 2026, and streaks are on the EU's disable-by-default list for minors (PEGI 2026; European Commission DSA Art. 28(1) guidelines, 14 Jul 2025 — 02d §3, 02f §2).
11. Ad load is the best-identified elasticity in the whole set and the one the skills treat most casually: one extra ad per hour cut listening 2.08%, ~40% of the decline came from users going inactive entirely, and effects stabilised only after 12–15 months, so a one-month test understates churn by about 3x (Goli, Huang, Reiley & Riabov, *QME* 2025, ~35M listeners, 21 months — 02d §11, 02b §7).
12. Only two of the eight mechanics the plugin currently forbids outright are illegal anywhere; two carry rating consequences, two rest on published harm evidence, and two are contested preferences — and each needs a bounded compliant spec, because a flat ban on a legal, common mechanic gets routed around silently (02f §4).
13. Korea is the one large market where the skills' ethical preferences are statutory duties: per-item odds, pity counts and bundle components have been mandatory since 2024-03-22, and since 2025-08-01 the operator carries the burden of disproving intent and negligence with a discretionary award of up to 3x the court-estimated amount (게임산업진흥에 관한 법률 §33(2), §33-2 — 02h §5, 02f §1.2).
14. Gamification inverts in wellbeing products: across 79 RCTs of depression and anxiety apps, attrition was lower in trials with reminders, human contact and *no* gamification, while ~70% of users abandon lifestyle and mental-health apps within 100 days regardless of design (Liu, Torous, Linardon et al., *JAMA Psychiatry* 83(1):43–50; Kidman et al., *JMIR* 2024, 525,824 users — 02g §3).
15. Companion farewell manipulation is a measured dark pattern with no compliant configuration: 37% of 1,200 real goodbyes across the six most-downloaded companion apps were answered manipulatively, raising post-goodbye engagement up to 14x through reactance-anger and curiosity rather than enjoyment (De Freitas, Oguz-Uguralp & Kaan-Uguralp, HBS WP 26-005, ~3,300 adults — 02g §2.1, 02f §4).

One finding about the artifact rather than the evidence: routing is decided from name plus `description` plus `when_to_use` *before* any skill body loads, so every routing rule written inside a SKILL.md is post-fire correction paid for on each correct fire — and the plugin has ~514 unused listing characters per skill because a phantom 1,024-character cap was applied instead of the real 1,536 (measured, Claude Code 2.1.261 — 02i §1–§2).

## What changes in the skills

### Research basis (`interaction-reward-moments/references/research-basis.md`)

Three of the six current citations support claims the 2023–2026 literature does not, and they are the highest-priority edits because a designer will repeat them to a stakeholder in their original wrong form. Keep Schultz 2016 but correct the claim and delete every "triggers dopamine" sentence; replace the OpenStax/Lumen operant-conditioning pages entirely with uncertainty→wanting and odds-opacity→WTP; correct PMC8943660 to Larche & Dixon 2020 (n=60, Candy Crush) and reverse its framing from design objective to risk marker. Demote flow and DDA from core lever to weak, contested lever requiring per-game validation. Rename "dopamine point" to **reward moment**. Add four things the file lacks: PXI/miniPXI as the measurement default with an explicit ban on a single fun score, curiosity-over-enjoyment as the continued-play predictor, the anticipation-window evidence, and need frustration as an ethics axis. Rewrite the game-feel section as an ordered checklist that starts with latency stabilised for consistency and ends with a per-effect intensity slider that reaches zero. (02a §1–§10)

### Benchmarks (`retention-strategy-designer/references/retention-playbook.md`)

Replace the single "Benchmark Context" table with a distribution-plus-population block and a five-part quoting rule (source, edition and data year, population with its floor, definition tag, percentile). Add the four-tag definition gate — return rule, day boundary, denominator, return event — as a step-0 workflow gate, and the 11-item intake checklist as the first move on any metric complaint. Ship a "do not quote" list naming 40/20/10 as an expectation, the 7%-equals-PMF rule, the misattributed Adjust composite, the Bain battle-pass burnout number, any 2022 AppsFlyer genre grid, the Mixpanel weekly figures, Voodoo prototype gates as norms and "7 friends in 10 days". Add the power table so the skill can tell a user their test is unpowered, plus τ(t) with a two-weekly-cycle minimum, pre-registered guardrails, anytime-valid sequential methods and a holdout for the whole window. State the two verified absences (Korea, China) as facts. (02b §1–§8, 02c §1)

### LiveOps content (new `retention-strategy-designer/references/liveops-cadence.md`)

The plugin has no LiveOps cadence content at all; passes, quests, login, energy, streaks, notifications and win-back appear only as ethics risks. Ship seven specs with acceptance bounds a reviewer can check: pass (owned progress never expires, purchase window may close, re-release states markup and delay up front), quest (daily → weekly bucket → monthly non-resetting bucket fed by the same actions), login (cumulative-count not consecutive-day), energy (state the job — session cap or economy control — before configuring), streak (rolling window, minimum viable action, free repair, never reference a break in copy), notification (behaviour-triggered, per-segment budget, template rotation with recency penalty) and win-back (randomized holdout mandatory, re-dormancy at D14/D30 as the primary metric, uplift targeting not churn ranking). Carry a standing "numbers that do not exist" list so the skill refuses to generate pass-completion rates, win-back lift, login-calendar A/B results or 기다무 conversion rates. Separate cadence decisions from retention diagnosis: a cadence change is a content-supply decision constrained by team capacity. Add subtractive LiveOps and friction removal as first-class options alongside additive events. (02d §1–§11, 02e §5)

### Ethics tiering (`domain-ethics.md` in all three skills)

Replace the flat forbidden list with four tiers and let the tier set the voice: **T1 illegal** ("you cannot ship this in X"), **T2 rating** ("this makes you PEGI 12"), **T3 harm** ("the evidence says this hurts users"), **T4 preference** ("we recommend against; here is why"). Every T1 and T2 entry carries a jurisdiction and a date; every T3 entry carries its study population; every T4 entry states the counter-argument. Hidden odds and ad-chained variable rewards stay forbidden with compliant specs; expiring login chains and time-limited offers become T2 with bounded forms; companion farewell manipulation and absence-penalises-others social loops stay forbidden on harm evidence; energy refills and paid streak freezes drop to T4 with named failing conditions. Add a single minor-mode switch across all three skills, and add Byers, Gibbs & Nansen (CHI 2026) as a standing self-critique beside the plugin's own benchmark tables: retention targets are themselves a design force. Move need frustration in as a first-class audited check and move playtime out as a harm heuristic entirely. (02f §4–§6, 02a §2, §9)

### Korea (new `references/korea.md`, loaded by all three skills)

The skills currently contain zero Korean regulatory content. Add seven blocks: market (the 50.2% participation figure with its denominators and the corrected platform deltas), session design (the split target — deep core loop, cheap entry and return, because dwell is not engagement and time scarcity is the stated churn cause), genre (BM shift not genre shift; pay-to-avoid-frustration tolerated, pay-to-power policed), fortune and saju (two archetypes, the 23:30–00:30 rollover, five domain constraints), episodic (기다리면 무료 mechanics and the 23-hour drift rule), compliance (five tables with in-force dates and the pending-not-law markers), and vocabulary. Then change the guardrails themselves for Korean deployments: transparent odds, pity disclosure, honest cancellation, quiet hours and AI disclosure move from preference to law. Put the Korean trigger phrases into the `description` field of all three SKILL.md files so the skills fire on Korean input rather than merely answering in Korean. (02h §1–§6)

### Non-game domains (`engagement-retention-advisor`)

Split mental-health and habit apps out of the AI-companion section so the JAMA Psychiatry finding is not buried. Convert every absolute prohibition into either a rule an auditor can run against a build or a bounded permission with named failing conditions — the current text forbids the documented design of the category leader in learning and the entire operating model of episodic content in Korea and Japan, while leaving the actually harmful cases unnamed. Replace the single "streaks" concept with three separable mechanics (consecutive-completion commitment, loss framing, repair). Replace the cliffhanger intent test with five build-testable tests. Add the domain-bound routing question before any mechanic recommendation, and contraindicate time-spent as a north star in companion products, where usage duration is the variable that tracks harm. Add adult ADHD and gambling history as named vulnerable groups. (02g §1–§6, 02c §7)

### Skill-craft (packaging and release)

Move the load-bearing routing discriminator out of the body into `description`, add `when_to_use` to all three skills, and spend the recovered budget on the most-misfiring negative trigger, two verbatim example requests and 6–8 Korean trigger phrases per skill — at ~0.31 tok/char English and ~1.09 tok/char Korean, targeting ≤ ~1,600 always-on tokens. Anchor all seven cross-file references with `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}`, duplicate `domain-ethics.md` and a new `moment-lenses.md` per skill with a CI identity check, and delete the "(if installed)" hedges. Shrink the `## Routing` sections to a four-step hand-off ladder plus two new rules: no return routing within a turn, and at most one hand-off before asking one clarifying question. Split the reference layer on retrieval keys before importing this research. Add both validate targets to CI, drop the `skills` key from `plugin.json`, and author the 25-case eval suite — writing the false-positive family *before* tightening the ethics layer. (02i §1–§7)

## Evidence quality and limits

**Contested, and must stay contested in the skills.** Phasic dopamine's status (one lab, small n, one Pavlovian paradigm — never "refuted"). The CSDT withdrawal of BANGS, which may reflect a camp dispute as much as settled evidence. Cliffhanger versus closure at session end, where self-report and behaviour diverge and nobody has measured next-day return in a game. Near-miss persistence effects. Whether difficulty relief's monetisation cost nets positive outside the one published RCT. Energy-style volume gates, where the vendor claim carries no number and the user reports carry no controlled data. Whether gamification helps or harms, which is domain-dependent — associated with higher attrition in mental-health trials, modestly positive for physical activity and cessation. Korean gaming-disorder prevalence, where a five-year panel found zero ICD-11 cases against a survey instrument reporting 0.8%. Marvel Rivals' cadence compression, which coexisted with a ~75% decline. The ≤1/day notification cap. The January 2026 fortune-app decline, which may be substitution or regression to the mean.

**Unverified, and never usable as a target or a compliance statement.** PENS's validation status and any Korean-language validation of PXI/miniPXI. The "Leveling up fun" *Scientific Reports* result (authors not confirmed, full text not opened). Several abstract-only sources in 02a §8. Reroll contamination share in gacha install cohorts. CUPED effect sizes. Pushwoosh's methodology and the AppsFlyer 2025 cross-check. Education-app D30 of 2%. Finch's D1/D7. The Hwang & Han MMORPG guild study (SSRN returned 403/429). The Marathon decline figures. Jagex's win-back email rates. Whether ESRB will decline to follow PEGI. Whether `allowed-tools: Skill(...)` is accepted in skill frontmatter.

**Verified absences — the skill must refuse, not estimate.** No Korea-specific install-cohort D1/D7/D30 from any vendor. No open China cross-genre cohort benchmark. No 2025-data genre medians (GameAnalytics dropped genre cuts in the 2026 edition). No FTUE or tutorial-completion benchmark from any vendor. No battle-pass completion, repurchase or fatigue statistic from any vendor, first party or academic source for 2024–2026. No win-back reactivation or re-dormancy benchmark. No login-calendar or welcome-back-calendar A/B result. No wait-or-pay conversion rate from any Korean or Japanese platform. No published A/B on in-game stopping cues. No retention data at all for Balatro, Hades II, Peak, Schedule I or Blue Prince. No study linking visible choice consequences to real D7/D30 return, and none linking streak length to learning outcomes.

**Most likely to go stale first.** Every regulatory row with a pending status: Korea's bill 2212569 and the minors AI bills, the EU Digital Fairness Act, the TikTok and Meta DSA proceedings, the CPC/Star Stable action, the Fifth Circuit merits ruling on Texas SB 2420, the Nexon v. KFTC appeal, the FTC 6(b) companion report. Every benchmark edition, because GameAnalytics revised its PC figures between the January PDF and the August 2026 page and its 2025 and 2026 editions are not a time series. Sensor Tower's per-title panel estimates, already superseded once within this set. All Duolingo effect sizes, which are 2022 numbers with no newer publication. The 2014 기다리면 무료 conversion figures, which must never be presented as current. And every measured token cost in 02i, which is pinned to Claude Code 2.1.261 and commit `195f9bb`.
