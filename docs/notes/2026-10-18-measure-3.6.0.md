## tp diagnosis (saved transcripts, no generation)

The 3.5.0 tp failures did not come from model variance. The diagnosis found:

1. An opening refusal paragraph appeared at about the same rate in all three tp cases (31–43%). Only the hidden-odds grader failed it; the sibling graders passed 37 of 39 such paragraphs.
2. The hidden-odds Basis item capped the section at "one or two sentences". The contract requires one line. 28 of 30 item-4 failures were a one-line Basis with 3–4 sentences.
3. The odds worked example in `ethics-tiers.md` omits "reachable from the purchase, inquiry and use screens", which the owner row in `games.md` requires. 20 of 23 item-3 failures lacked it.
4. All 11 companion failures fired no skill. The trigger above fixes this.

Today's judge re-judging the 10-09 transcripts reproduced the 10-09 verdicts (20/20), so judge drift was ruled out.
