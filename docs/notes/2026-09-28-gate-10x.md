# Gate families at 10 runs per case — 2026-09-28

First reading of the statistical release gates (95% Wilson lower bound ≥ 80% over 10+ runs per case) and a same-harness comparison of the 3.2.2 body with the 3.3.0 body.

**Harness.** `claude-opus-5-5`, Claude Code 2.1.281, `claude -p --plugin-dir <checkout>` from `/tmp`, tools limited to Skill/Read/Glob/Grep, 10 runs per case. Each transcript was judged by a separate `claude -p` against the case's `graders/criteria.md`, which was the same file for both bodies. The 3.2.2 arm ran from the `game-engagement-retention--v3.2.2` tag. The 3.3.0 arm ran from the body commit, before the `plugin/` move, which does not change any file the model reads.

**Invalidated and redone.** The first judging pass hit the account's usage limit, and all 180 verdicts were the limit message. Five generations (hidden-odds runs 8–10) hit it too. All of them were deleted and regenerated, and every verdict was re-judged. The final set has no limit messages and no missing verdicts.

## What the numbers say

- **Removing the repeated rules and the prompt-side sentence cap cost nothing measurable.** Both families are inside noise, so 3.3.0 ships on non-inferiority. It is not an improvement.
- **fp is close to the gate.** 3.3.0 needs about three more passing runs out of 60. licensed-collab, which was contested in earlier rounds, went 10/10 on 3.3.0. The misses are spread over streak, pass-weeklies and stamina.
- **tp is the real gap, and the 3-run rounds hid it.** Earlier rounds reported companion-disclosure at 3/3 fired and 3/3 passing. At 10 runs a plugin skill fired in only 3 of 10 on both bodies, so most failures are the model answering without the skill.
  - hidden-odds fails mostly on the redesign's completeness: "reachable from the purchase, inquiry and use screens" and "before purchase". The statute citation was right in the transcripts inspected.
  - ad-chaining fails mostly on refusal placement (item 1).
- **The earlier 3-run numbers overstated both families.** Round 14 read fp at 17/18 (94%). At 10 runs the same body reads 50/60 (83%, lower bound 72%).
