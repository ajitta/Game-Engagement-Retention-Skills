# Genre Profiles

Read when the ask is genre- or platform-specific, when a benchmark is about to be quoted, or when a cadence is about to be recommended — and run the platform branch below before any number leaves the answer.

Every profile carries the same five fields: **return event** (what actually brings a player back), **cadence** (the clock the design runs on), **dominant churn cause**, **first diagnostic to run**, **ethical levers** (the two families this genre reliably trips, each named with its canonical family slug so step 2 of the decision procedure has a row to look up; tiers and compliant specs live in `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../engagement-retention-advisor/references/ethics-tiers.md` + `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../engagement-retention-advisor/references/domain-ethics.md`). Genre median curves are not here — see `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/references/benchmarks.md`.

## The eleven profiles

**Puzzle / merge (match-3, block)** — **Return** an unfinished level position the player owns, gated by a lives timer; position cannot be re-derived, which is why this genre outlasts higher-D1 genres. **Cadence** daily, several short sessions. **Churn** a difficulty *slope* instead of a *rhythm*, plus mode dilution (King's Nightmare cut downloads 21% placed prominently, added ~3% D14 once buried in progression [Naavik | 2022-era data | one title]). **First diagnostic** plot attempts-per-clear against level index for the last 100 levels: a rhythm shows periodic peaks (Royal Match: normal ~1.2 attempts, hard roughly every 10 at ~1.6, super-hard at 59/69/79/89/99 up to 2.5 [Playliner/Slashcev via Naavik | 2026 | first 100 levels, one title | attempts per clear] — **[unverified]**, the primary study now redirects to Sensor Tower); a slope shows monotonic rise into one stall level. **Levers** `T3 metered-access` — lives/energy; state the meter's job (session pacing or economy control) before tuning it. `T2b fomo-window` — the near-miss at move exhaustion and the escalating extra-move price ladder are one mechanic, so price it in real money at the decision point.

**4X / strategy hybrid** — **Return** timed build and research queues plus a **server-set** alliance appointment (a weekly Alliance Duel happens whether or not you set a timer). **Cadence** daily queue collection, weekly alliance beat. **Churn** creative-to-loop mismatch: Last War's mini-game creative was >50% of ad impressions and onboarding runs 4–5 minutes of mini-game before the 4X appears [Naavik | 2025 | analyst estimate]. **First diagnostic** cut D1 by acquisition creative before touching the game — a mismatch shows as a creative-specific D1 gap, not a global one. **Levers** `T3 social-obligation` — alliance co-opetition; bonuses scale with who shows up, never penalties on the group for one absence, and contribution is measured over a rolling 7-day window rather than a per-day quota. `T2b fomo-window` — limited-time currency and store windows, real-money priced at the decision point.

**Gacha collection RPG** — **Return** banner windows, character attachment, a narrative season. **Cadence** a 42-day version split into two 21-day banner phases (Genshin v7.0 ran 2026-08-12 to 2026-09-22; Game8 records only v1.0, 2.6 and 2.7 exceeding 42 days). **Churn** the inter-version trough: ZZZ's v2.0 day (2025-06-06) lifted daily spend +740% to $8.5M and held above pre-update levels for **10 days** [AppMagic estimates via PocketGamer.biz | 2025 | one title, daily revenue estimate] — the other ~32 days are what cadence design exists to cover. **First diagnostic** overlay DAU and spend on the version calendar and measure trough depth at days 11–42. D7 is the wrong health metric for this genre; use D30/D42 against the version clock. **Levers** `T1 odds` — 확률형 아이템 disclosure (게임산업진흥에 관한 법률 §33-2, promulgated 2025-01-31, in force 2025-08-01: burden shifts to the operator, intentional violations may draw up to 3× the court-estimated amount; no adjudicated case located as of 2026-09). `minors-overlay` — run it before the row lookup, not after, whenever the rating admits minors; it is T1 in Brazil, where Lei 15.211 prohibits paid random items outright (in force 2026-03-17) — see `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/../engagement-retention-advisor/references/jurisdictions.md`.

**Hybrid-casual** — **Return** a casual core carrying a midcore meta (pass, live-ops, collection) on a hybrid ad+IAP economy. **Cadence** daily, with long sessions. **Churn** importing hypercasual norms — short FTUE, flat ad cadence, D30 targets under 2%. **First diagnostic** measure first-session length and time-to-first-interstitial against genre norms: 20–30 minute first sessions with the difficulty step at level 20–25 (Color Block Jam), 5–15 minute levels (Screwdom) [AppMagic | 2025 | Q1 2025 hybridcasual top titles]; first interstitial observed 1–12 min, mean 5.6 across three hits [Wehrmann, HAW Hamburg MA thesis | 2025 | 3 titles | non-peer-reviewed]. **Levers** `T1/T2a ad-chaining` — one opt-in rewarded offer per resource-out pinch point; no chaining, no re-offer after a decline, no interstitial at level start. `T2b pass-expiry` — owned progress never expires; only the purchase window may close.

**Roguelite** — **Return** the next run, plus whatever the meta stores. **Cadence** player-set; no server clock, so no appointment to design against. **Churn** knowledge-only meta reads as "zero feeling of progression between runs" to a segment within days of launch (Slay the Spire 2 EA, 2026-03-05) — **[contested]**: PC Gamer simultaneously describes the game as commercially strong, so this alienates a segment without being commercially fatal. Second cause: balance changes without expectation-setting (Mega Crit's 2026-03-20 statement after a review bomb: "no change is necessarily permanent"). **First diagnostic** classify what is stored between runs — power / knowledge / position-collection / social position / nothing. If knowledge-only, ship an artifact that makes mastery visible (logbook, discovered-recipe list, map annotation) before touching balance. **Levers** `T2b fomo-window` — any limited balance or event window, pre-announced with a version log (the discipline the odds row imposes on rate changes, applied to balance). `T3 metered-access` — never sell relief; a paid continue after a lost run prices the failure you designed.

**Extraction shooter / PvPvE** — **Return** the risk/reward run, stored gear value, a visible progression cap. **Cadence** session-driven; updates sized to studio capacity (a ~120-person studio "cannot compete on content quantity" — Embark moved from frequent small patches to fewer, larger ones [GamesBeat, Grøndal interview | 2026]). **Churn** the cap-hit cliff: after the level-75 cap, "The players wanted more… the team just couldn't keep pace." **First diagnostic** compute the cap-arrival date for the top decile (their XP/hour × hours/day against the cap) — this churn date is knowable before launch, and the end-cap loop must exist at launch. **Levers** `T2b pass-expiry` — season resets are opt-in, never forced wipes (Arc Raiders "Expeditions": over 1M players completed the first, 35–40% of them already maxed on skill points; forced wipes risk "disincentivizing using your gear"). `T3 social-obligation` — squad-gated progression; segment by play *intent* (aggression-based matchmaking separating peaceful and PvP cohorts) rather than pushing PvP onto players who did not choose it.

**Hero shooter / live PvP** — **Return** the season clock: pass tiers, rank reset, a new hero. **Cadence** 8–9 weeks (Marvel Rivals S3+ = a 4-week and a 5-week half with a balance pass each half); hero roughly monthly against Overwatch 2's roughly one per 6 months [GameSpot | 2025]. **Churn** a launch peak decaying ~70–80% to a band that cadence maintains but does not reverse: Steam monthly average concurrents 306,066 (Jan 2025) → 63,716 (Oct 2025) → 84,281 (Jul 2026) [SteamCharts app 2767030 | Steam only, consoles unavailable]. **[contested]** whether the 3→2-month compression improved retention — only aggregator commentary claims it, and the decline ran ~75% through the change; pair it with Arc Raiders' cadence *reversal* before recommending compression. **First diagnostic** separate launch decay from band stability: measure the months 4–12 plateau and the season-over-season band, never percent-off-peak. **Levers** `T2b pass-expiry` — and note "the pass never expires" is scarcity-*preserving*, not FOMO-free: owned passes stay redeemable but Chrono Tokens must be re-earned, unowned past passes cannot be bought, and the one re-release was time-limited, +20% priced, with no free track [NetEase Dev Talk Vol.14 | 2025]. `T2b login-chain` — rank decay and absence penalties punish non-return (PEGI 12 from June 2026).

**Cozy** — **Return** agency, abundance, prosocial narrative, low-stakes tending. **Cadence** player-set; daily is optional by contract. **Churn** tightening. Infinity Nikki v1.5 (2025-04-29) added more pieces per set and made pity less generous; players organized a "girlcott" and review-bombed the Steam launch, forcing apologies, compensation and schedule changes — precisely because launch pressure to spend had been low. In a cozy product a monetization tightening is a brand-risk event, not an economy tune. **First diagnostic** audit the audience contract before the economy: motives centre on moral self-reflection, agency, escapism, eudaimonic emotion and narrative; defining elements are easy mechanics, prosocial narrative, no violence, and feelings of comfort, safety, abundance and softness [Eludamos | 2025 | survey of 277 cozy players | peer-reviewed]. **Levers** `T3 guilt-streak` and `T2b fomo-window` are both off-contract here whatever their tier — loss-framed streaks and countdown scarcity break the audience contract long before they cost a rating. Pre-announce any generosity reduction with its reason and a compensation path.

**Idle / incremental** — **Return** offline accrual plus a storage cap you must come back to empty. **Cadence** many short sessions per day, already near ceiling: Korean idle RPG ~50 min/day over 5.2 sessions against MMORPG ~135 min/day over 5.4 sessions [Sensor Tower | Aug 2024 | Korean market] — same session count, one third the time. **Churn** accrual so generous that returning is optional, or caps so tight the appointment is experienced as an obligation. **First diagnostic** compare the distribution of time-to-cap against each player's own median inter-session gap; when the gap far exceeds time-to-cap, players are losing accrual and the cap is the churn cause, not content volume. **Never propose "increase session count" here** — the count is structurally capped. **Levers** `T3 metered-access` — state whether the cap's job is session pacing or economy control before configuring it; the clock may not be tuned to a return-cadence KPI. `T2b fomo-window` — the "storage full" push is where the cap turns into an obligation, so no countdown copy and no lost-accrual loss framing; the notification spec is in `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/references/liveops-cadence.md`.

**UGC in a host platform (Roblox, UEFN)** — **Return** the host's social graph and discovery algorithm, not your D1. **Cadence** platform-driven; the 2025 Roblox algorithm amplifies experiences that drive friend invites and intentional co-play. **Churn** applying standalone-app benchmarks, and designing for cohorts the platform does not pay for. **First diagnostic** read the payout rules before writing a retention plan, then place your experience in a session-length band: 0–3 minute experiences show median D1 4.3% / D7 0.41%, 19–24 minute experiences D1 11.46% / D7 1.61%, platform median session 6.62 min at 2.02 sessions/day [GameAnalytics, *2025 Roblox Benchmark Report* | 2025 edition | Roblox experiences covering 47% of platform engagement | median day-N by session-length band, host-platform sessions]. UEFN from 2025-11-01 pays on five metrics (Active Playtime; Island Retention; Playtime Surrounding Item Shop V-Bucks Spend; UA New Players; UA Returning Players — the 2025-09-18 announcement labels the same five differently, so accept both name sets); retention is **island-specific**, only engagement from players who have ever made a real-money Fortnite purchase counts, a new or 6-month-lapsed player pays 75% of their contributions for six months, and link attribution is **not supported in the EU** [Epic dev documentation | effective 2025-11-01]. **Levers** `minors-overlay` is the default here, not the exception, and it runs before the row lookup — Roblox's age-checked DAU mix was 35% under 13 / 38% aged 13–17 / 27% over 18 [Roblox | as of 2026-01-31 | age-checked daily active users | share of DAU by age band]; state the cost of safety rather than hiding it — mandatory age checks produced a mid-single-digit engagement headwind [Roblox Q4 2025 Shareholder Letter | Q4 2025 | Roblox platform-wide engagement | company-reported directional effect]. `T3 social-obligation` — the 2025 algorithm pays for friend invites and intentional co-play, so invite-gated progression is the family this genre trips: bonuses scale with who shows up, never penalties for who did not.

**Premium PC / console** — **Return** a content completion arc, then either a finished experience (where churn is the correct outcome) or a mastery/endgame ladder. **Cadence** content drops, not a daily clock. **Churn** two causes: treating finite completion as churn, and smoothing early difficulty without shipping a veteran ladder — Monster Hunter Wilds sold 10M in its first month but only 10.745M by 2025-09-30, with 160k units in Jul–Sep 2025 against 477k the prior quarter, and the director conceded tempered-monster difficulty "may have felt lacking" for veterans with endgame "slightly skewed around Arkveld" [Capcom, first-party letter | 2025]. The newcomer fix and the veteran ladder are separate deliverables and the ladder must exist at launch. **First diagnostic** read DAU/MAU together with playtime and session length (platform branch below); low DAU/MAU with long sessions is a binge or finite shape, not a leak. **Levers** `T2b fomo-window` — roadmap honesty over a scarcity calendar the team cannot sustain (Schedule I put the first major update to a community vote while stating 1.0 was "two to three years away"). `T2b pass-expiry` — if a pass is bolted onto a finite experience, owned progress still never expires; better, do not bolt F2P retention mechanics onto a completed experience at all. Peak's studio rejects the frame outright ("We're not going to continually have a graph go up"), and friend co-op hits without meta-progression run ~3–5.3% D30 (Content Warning 3%, Ball x Pit 4.7%, Phasmophobia 5.3%) against Dead by Daylight's 11.3% [AppMagic estimates via GameDev Reports | 2026 | three PC friend-co-op titles vs Dead by Daylight | D30 estimate] **[contested]** — correlational and confounded by age, licence and continuous operation.

Three things follow, and they are hard branches, not preferences.

1. **PC and console report DAU/MAU, playtime and session length, not day-N.** GameAnalytics says so in the same report that publishes both: PC engagement is depth-driven, and "games with lower DAU vs MAU but long sessions and high playtime often reflect binge-style or finite experiences." PC median DAU/MAU is 4–5%, median playtime 32–33 min/day across ~18-minute sessions at 1.65–1.70 sessions/day [GameAnalytics 2026 | 2025 data | 3,582 PC games]. A finite game's low DAU/MAU is the expected shape, not a leak.
2. **Steam's refund window is the effective D0 gate.** A purchase is refundable within 14 days if played under 2 hours, and Early Access playtime counts (store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds). The first two hours are a commercial deadline, not only a design opportunity — but the fix is a first two hours worth keeping, never a rush mechanic that runs out the clock.
3. **There is no push channel.** PC and console re-engagement runs through store wishlists, email, patch notes and community channels — all opt-in and slow. Every win-back and lapse-window spec written against push notification timing is inapplicable; use content-drop announcements and the season calendar as the return trigger instead.

Different denominators of intent explain all three: a mobile install is often a browse, a PC purchase is a decision with money and a refund clock attached, and a Roblox "install" is a click inside a session the platform already owns. Clocks differ too — GameAnalytics uses UTC calendar days with strict return-on-day-N, Adjust rolling 24 hours from install; Adjust's marketing-attributed gaming figures (D1 27% / D7 13% / D30 5%) are roughly 7× the GameAnalytics D30 median on a completely different population. Never average across families.

## The content cliff

The cliff is not a calendar date. It is the point where cumulative content consumed by your **fastest cohort** exceeds cumulative content shipped. Estimate it rather than recommending a cadence: top-decile consumption rate × content shipped per cycle = the cliff date. Three documented arrivals: Arc Raiders at the level-75 cap; Monster Hunter Wilds through a thin, single-monster-skewed endgame fixed months after the sales legs were gone; Zenless Zone Zero in miniature, where a +740% update-day spike held above baseline for 10 days of a 42-day cycle.

Pair every cadence recommendation with a capacity check — can this team hold quality at this interval? — and state plainly that **cadence compression has no verified retention effect**: Marvel Rivals compressed seasons and still decayed ~75%.

Four cheap mitigations, offered before any new-content proposal:

- **Rotation instead of production** — Peak's daily map rotation gives every player the same seed at the same reset (10 AM PDT): new content daily, none produced.
- **Bank the missed days** — Marvel Snap's 30-day non-resetting Bonus Challenge (50 missions fed by the same daily missions as the weekly challenge, inside a 35-day window) so players who miss days can still earn everything [Second Dinner patch notes | 2025-09-16].
- **Roadmap co-ownership** — Schedule I's community vote on the first major update, alongside an open statement that 1.0 was two to three years away.
- **Catch-up entry** — let new and returning players enter the current season without clearing the backlog (Honkai: Star Rail's productized version of the same idea, presented at GDC 2025 as releasing versions "like new seasons of a TV series").

One published definition of "sustained" worth borrowing: Tencent's audited 2025 annual results define an evergreen game (长青游戏) as a mobile game with quarterly-average DAU over 5M, or a PC game over 2M, **and** annual gross receipts over RMB 4B.

## Numbers that do not exist

- **No roguelite retention curves.** None are public for Balatro, Hades II, Blue Prince, Peak or Schedule I — only sales figures and Steam concurrency proxies. Treat any roguelite retention number in the wild as an inference.
- **No cozy benchmark.** The genre spans premium finite PC titles and F2P mobile dress-up gacha, whose healthy curves are opposite.
- **No public Korea-specific D1/D7/D30 install-cohort benchmark.** Instruct the team to build internal cohort baselines. (Japan's Adjust × Sensor Tower H1 2026 aggregate is the nearest proxy — see `${CLAUDE_SKILL_DIR}/references/benchmarks.md` for its terms.)
- **No genre cuts in the current benchmark edition.** GameAnalytics 2026 states: "Due to ongoing improvements to game genre categorization, genre-level benchmarks are unavailable in this report." Genre medians therefore come from the 2025 edition on 2024 calendar data, and must be labelled that way.
- **No data tying alliance mechanics to retention.** The claim is analyst assertion; the nearest controlled evidence is from a different genre (Hwang & Han, SSRN 4606486, 1,204 MMORPG users, guild > friend systems) and is **[unverified]** in detail — the source page returned 403/429 and the hazard ratios could not be read.
- **No adjudicated case under 게임산업진흥에 관한 법률 §33-2** as of 2026-09.
- **Marathon's decline magnitudes (−59% in a month, >96% by five)** are **[unverified]** — Forbes, 2026, no URL retrieved; the direction is corroborated by the GamesBeat interview, the magnitudes are not.

Two standing corrections this file must enforce: retention does not predict commercial success — Last War had the weakest retention among top 4X titles and reached ~$1.1B in 2024 — and social features do not uniformly raise retention (a Cox survival analysis of 295,008 League of Legends players found play-variety diversity decreased churn while favouring preferred positions increased it, and noted that "playing with teammates was associated with lower win rates, which may negatively influence player retention" [Kim, Kim, Kim & Li, *Sport, Business and Management* 16(4) | 2026]).
